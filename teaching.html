Teaching philosophy

I came into academia as a first-generation student, and the professors who mentored me never set low expectations. They always thought I could do more and the best, regardless of my origins, and for me, that's the beauty of academia. They saw that I was obsessed with certain ideas and tools for some obscure reason (even to me, to some extent), and they just cherished this fact and made everything possible for me to think carefully through ideas and learn all the required tools to express them. This is how I have thought about my teaching philosophy.
