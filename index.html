About

I am a Post-doctoral Research Fellow at the London Business School, working on the economics of information systems.

I completed my Ph.D. in Management Information Systems at the Indian Institute of Management Calcutta (2021–2026). My current research examines how digital technologies can contribute to sustainable development in emerging contexts, with a particular focus on digital infrastructure investments, public-sector digital reforms, and digital platforms. I am trained in quasi-experimental research designs, with experience analyzing large-scale survey, administrative, and geospatial data.

Research interests

Economics of IS, Societal Impact of Digital Technologies, and Public-sector Digital Reforms
